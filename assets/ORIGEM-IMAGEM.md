# Origem da base visual

Arquivo: `aerial-demo-v2.png`, 1536 × 1024 pixels. Produzido com geração de imagem integrada (imagegen), em 29/09/2026. Ilustração sintética de paisagem rural brasileira fictícia, não fotografia de propriedade identificada, nem imagem de sensoriamento remoto real. Sem resolução espacial, escala ou georreferenciamento. Os vetores foram desenhados separadamente no aplicativo.

Prompt final utilizado:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed basemap texture for a geospatial engineering DEMONSTRATION web app. Generate one extremely detailed realistic orthographic satellite / aerial nadir photograph of a fictional Brazilian cerrado agricultural property and surroundings, landscape 1536x1024 or higher. Looks like genuine high-resolution orthophotography at around 1m/px, absolutely NOT a vector drawing or low-poly or illustration. Top down with no horizon or perspective, clear atmosphere. Large patchwork tan harvested fields and olive green planted fields with fine parallel tractor lines, irregular richly textured dark forest / cerrado remnant covering the central left to lower center, a narrow sinuous creek running diagonally from upper middle to lower right with branching riparian corridors, a thin pale dirt access road across upper third and on the left edge, tiny rural building group far upper-right. Land parcels with realistic rectilinear boundaries interrupted by riparian forest, intricate varied tree canopies and subtle elevations. Crisp but naturally photographic, natural muted forest greens / red-brown soil / straw ochre, not neon. Composition: surrounding landscape extends fully to all edges; no framing or UI. NO lines drawn, no markings, no labels, no words, no compass, no legend, no map pins, no grid, no watermark. This will be clearly labeled as synthetic illustration in the app and must not purport to document any specific real property.

A menção a 1m/px no prompt orienta somente o estilo visual. Não atribui resolução espacial ao resultado.
